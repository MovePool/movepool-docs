# How It Works

MovePool is a **prize savings protocol**—think of it as a savings account where instead of earning interest directly, you get lottery tickets for prize draws.

## The Core Concept

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/how-it-works.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Deposit → Tickets → Prize Draws → Withdraw</p></figcaption></figure>

### 1️⃣ Deposit

When you deposit MOVE tokens, they go into a shared vault. Your deposit is tracked as **shares** representing your portion of the pool.

### 2️⃣ Get Tickets

Your shares are converted to **lottery tickets**:

```
1 MOVE = 1 Ticket
```

The more you deposit, the higher your chances of winning.

### 3️⃣ Earn Yield

The vault generates yield over time. This yield is **not** given to depositors—instead, it funds the prize pools.

### 4️⃣ Prize Draws

Draws happen automatically:

- **Quick Draw**: Every week, 70% of yield
- **Jackpot**: Every month, 30% of yield

Winners are selected randomly, weighted by ticket count.

### 5️⃣ Withdraw Anytime

Your **principal is always safe**. Withdraw your full deposit at any time—only the yield is used for prizes.

---

## Why Zero-Loss?

Traditional lotteries take your money permanently. MovePool is different:

| Traditional Lottery           | MovePool                       |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| ❌ Money is spent             | ✅ Deposit is returned         |
| ❌ 100% loss if you don't win | ✅ 0% loss—only yield at risk  |
| ❌ House always wins          | ✅ Users always keep principal |

---

## The Math

If the vault holds **10,000 MOVE** and generates **12% APY**:

- Annual yield: **1,200 MOVE**
- Weekly yield (Quick Draw): ~**16 MOVE** (70% of weekly)
- Monthly yield (Jackpot): ~**30 MOVE** (30% of monthly)

These prizes go to random winners while everyone's deposit stays safe.
