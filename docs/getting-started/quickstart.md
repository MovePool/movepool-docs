# Quickstart

Get started with MovePool in under 5 minutes.

## Prerequisites

- A wallet connected to Movement Testnet
- Some testnet MOVE tokens (get them from the [faucet](https://faucet.movementnetwork.xyz))

## Step 1: Visit the App

Go to [movepool.vercel.app](https://movepool.vercel.app)

## Step 2: Connect Wallet

1. Click **Get Started** or **Launch App**
2. Sign in with Email or Google (Privy creates an embedded wallet for you)
3. Your wallet address will appear in the dashboard

## Step 3: Fund Your Wallet

If your wallet is empty, you'll see a banner prompting you to fund it.

1. Copy your wallet address
2. Visit the [Movement Faucet](https://faucet.movementnetwork.xyz)
3. Paste your address and request tokens
4. Wait ~30 seconds for tokens to arrive

## Step 4: Make Your First Deposit

1. Navigate to **Deposit**
2. Enter an amount (e.g., 1 MOVE)
3. Choose **Quick Draw** (weekly) or **Jackpot** (monthly)
4. Click **Deposit & Enter**
5. Confirm the transaction

{% hint style="success" %}
**You're in!** You now have lottery tickets for the next draw.
{% endhint %}

## Step 5: Check Your Position

Your dashboard shows:

- **Balance**: Your share of the vault
- **Tickets**: Entries in each pool
- **Prizes Won**: Total winnings

## Next Steps
