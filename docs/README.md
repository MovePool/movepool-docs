## ✨ App Features

### Two Prize Pools

| Pool           | Draw Frequency | Prize Share  |
| -------------- | -------------- | ------------ |
| **Quick Draw** | Weekly         | 70% of yield |
| **Jackpot**    | Monthly        | 30% of yield |

### Gamification

- **XP & Levels** — Earn points for every action
- **Daily Streaks** — Bonus multipliers for consistency
- **Badges** — Permanent on-chain achievements
- **Leaderboard** — Compete for monthly rankings

### Referrals

Invite friends and earn **+5 bonus tickets** when they deposit!

## How It Works

1. **Deposit** MOVE tokens into the vault
2. **Get Tickets** proportional to your deposit (1 MOVE = 1 ticket)
3. **Win Prizes** funded by yield—not your principal
4. **Withdraw Anytime** with your full deposit intact

[Learn more →](getting-started/how-it-works.md)
