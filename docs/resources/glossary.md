# Glossary

Key terms used in MovePool documentation.

## A

### APY (Annual Percentage Yield)

The yearly return rate on deposits. MovePool currently simulates 12% APY.

### Auto-Enter

Feature that automatically re-enters users into the next prize draw.

## D

### Draw

The lottery event where winners are selected and prizes distributed.

## J

### Jackpot

The monthly prize pool receiving 30% of yield.

## M

### MOVE

The native token of Movement Network, used for deposits and prizes.

### Movement Network

The Layer 2 blockchain where MovePool is deployed.

## P

### Principal

The original deposit amount. In MovePool, principal is never at risk.

### PPS (Price Per Share)

The current value of one vault share in MOVE. Only increases over time.

### Prize Pool

The accumulated yield available for prize distribution.

## Q

### Quick Draw

The weekly prize pool receiving 70% of yield.

## R

### Reserve

The token balance backing prize payouts. Prizes can only be paid from funded reserves.

### Referral

System where existing users earn bonus tickets by inviting new users.

## S

### Shares

Units representing ownership of vault deposits. Shares are exchanged for MOVE on withdrawal.

## T

### Tickets

Lottery entries. 1 MOVE deposit = 1 ticket.

## V

### Vault

The smart contract holding all user deposits and managing share accounting.

## Y

### Yield

Returns generated by the vault. In MovePool, yield funds prizes instead of going directly to depositors.

## Z

### Zero-Loss

The core guarantee: user principal is never at risk, only yield is used for prizes.
