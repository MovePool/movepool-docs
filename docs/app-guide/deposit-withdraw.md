# Deposit & Withdraw

MovePool lets you deposit and withdraw at any time. Your principal is always safe.

---

## Depositing

1. Navigate to **Deposit** in the sidebar
2. Enter the amount of MOVE you want to deposit
3. Select a pool:
   - **Quick Draw** — 7-day lock, weekly prizes
   - **Jackpot** — 30-day lock, bigger monthly prizes
4. Click **Deposit & Enter**
5. Confirm the transaction

{% hint style="success" %}
**Tickets Earned:** 1 MOVE = 1 ticket. The more you deposit, the better your odds!
{% endhint %}

### What Happens to Your Deposit?

- Your MOVE is deposited into the vault
- You receive "shares" representing your position
- Yield is generated and added to prize pools
- Your principal stays intact

---

## Withdrawing

1. Navigate to **Withdraw**
2. Enter the amount to withdraw (or click **Max**)
3. Click **Confirm Withdraw**
4. Receive your MOVE tokens back

{% hint style="info" %}
**No Penalties:** Withdraw anytime with your full principal + any prizes won.
{% endhint %}

### Partial Withdrawals

You can withdraw part of your balance. Remaining funds continue to earn tickets.

---

## Transaction History

All deposits and withdrawals appear in your **Recent Activity** feed on the dashboard.
