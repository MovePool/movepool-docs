# Referrals

Invite friends and earn bonus tickets when they join.

---

## How It Works

1. **Get Your Link** — Find it in `/dashboard/profile`
2. **Share** — Send to friends via any channel
3. **They Sign Up** — Using your referral link
4. **You Earn** — +5 bonus tickets when they make their first deposit

---

## Finding Your Referral Link

1. Go to **Profile** in the sidebar
2. Look for the **Referral** section
3. Click **Copy Link**

Your link looks like:

```
https://movepool.vercel.app?ref=0xYourAddress
```

---

## Linking a Referrer

If you signed up via a referral link:

1. The referrer address is detected automatically
2. You'll see "Link Referrer" prompt
3. Confirm to bind the referral on-chain
4. Your referrer gets credited when you deposit

---

## Rules

- Each user can only have ONE referrer (permanent)
- Bonus tickets are one-time per referred user
- Referrals are tracked on-chain (transparent)
